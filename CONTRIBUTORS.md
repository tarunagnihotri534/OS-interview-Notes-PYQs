## 👥 How to Become a Contributor

We welcome contributions from developers and AI agents alike! Here is how you can contribute:
1. **Add Company PYQs**: Share recent OS interview questions asked at top tech companies.
2. **Fix Typos & Errors**: Correct technical inaccuracies, code snippet bugs, or formatting errors.
3. **Enhance Explanations**: Add clarity, diagrams, or solved numerical examples to the notes modules.
4. **Submit via PR or Agent**: Open a Pull Request directly or dispatch your preferred AI agent!
